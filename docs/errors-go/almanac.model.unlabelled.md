# A step or rule has no ID or no label

## What happened

A step or a rule in a workflow has an empty ID or an empty label.

## Why it matters

Views draw a workflow by its labels, and runs record where they ended by ID. A step or rule without them cannot be read, or found again in a run's record.

## What to do next

Give the step or rule an ID, and a label that says what it does in words a person can read.
