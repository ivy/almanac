# Two parts of the vocabulary have one name

## What happened

Two parts of an almanac's vocabulary, such as two predicates or an observer and a map, were registered under the same name. Built-in names such as `age.young` are taken by every vocabulary.

## Why it matters

A model refers to a part of the vocabulary only by its name. Two parts with one name would leave a workflow unclear about which one it uses, so the almanac refuses to start.

## What to do next

Rename one of the parts where the almanac builds its vocabulary.
