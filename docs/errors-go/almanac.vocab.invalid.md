# A part of the vocabulary cannot be registered

## What happened

A part of the vocabulary has a name that is not lowercase words joined by dots, has no label, or has a type that breaks a rule: an observer or event that would hold text that is not hostile, a worker whose answer is not clean, or a classifier whose answers are not distinct.

## Why it matters

The vocabulary is what every workflow model combines, and its labels are what views show. A part that cannot be named, read or typed safely would weaken every workflow that used it.

## What to do next

Fix the registration the message names, where the almanac builds its vocabulary.
